# Rise of the Plants — Polish & Buildathon Optimization Pass

The core architecture, gameplay loop, Sanity integration, backend, and persistence systems are complete and approved.

Do NOT perform architecture refactors.

Do NOT redesign the data model.

Do NOT replace existing systems.

Do NOT introduce new major dependencies.

Your task is now to maximize Buildathon judging impact and demo quality.

---

## Primary Goal

Optimize Rise of the Plants for a 2–3 minute judge experience.

A judge should be able to:

1. Open the game
2. Understand the concept immediately
3. Complete a task
4. Grow a plant
5. Trigger an evolution
6. Unlock a new species
7. Read Codex lore loaded from Sanity
8. Win a battle
9. Open AR mode
10. Leave impressed

The project should feel magical, polished, and memorable.

---

# Priority 1 — Evolution Experience

The evolution moment must become the strongest moment in the game.

Enhance evolution presentation using existing systems.

Add:

- Glow effects
- Particle effects
- Scale animation
- Smooth transformation animation
- New rarity reveal
- Species discovery celebration
- Codex unlock feedback
- XP burst effect
- Level-up celebration

Evolution should feel rewarding and dramatic.

Goal:

A judge should immediately recognize:

"This is a major progression event."

Do not make it feel like a simple level increase.

---

# Priority 2 — Discovery Experience

When a new species is unlocked:

Create a dedicated discovery reveal sequence.

Display:

- Species artwork
- Species name
- Rarity
- Short lore excerpt
- Discovery status
- Button to view Codex

Presentation should resemble a collectible creature discovery moment.

The player should feel rewarded for progression.

---

# Priority 3 — Codex Polish

The Codex is one of the strongest demonstrations of Sanity.

Improve presentation.

Ensure every Codex entry clearly displays:

- Artwork
- Name
- Rarity
- Habitat
- Description
- Lore
- Evolution information

Portable Text content from Sanity should render beautifully.

Add a small informational note:

"Content dynamically powered by Sanity."

This should be tasteful and not intrusive.

The goal is to make Sanity usage obvious to judges.

---

# Priority 4 — Collection Experience

- Clear rarity indicators
