# Rise of the Plants (ROP)

## Game Design Document

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Game Overview

Rise of the Plants (ROP) is a web-first creature collection game where players nurture magical plant beings through real-world-inspired actions.

Players gather resources, grow plant creatures, unlock evolutions, discover lore, view plants in augmented reality, and battle AI opponents.

The game is designed around short, rewarding gameplay sessions and a strong sense of discovery.

---

# 2. Design Goals

## Goal 1: Discovery

Players should constantly feel there is another plant waiting to be discovered.

---

## Goal 2: Growth

Every action should create visible progress.

Players should feel attached to their plants.

---

## Goal 3: Wonder

AR experiences should create memorable moments.

---

## Goal 4: Simplicity

The game should be understandable within minutes.

---

## Goal 5: Collection

Players should be motivated to complete their Codex.

---

# 4. Player Fantasy

The player is a magical botanist exploring a hidden ecosystem of sentient plants.

Every species has:

* Unique origins
* Evolution paths
* Habitats
* Stories
* Personalities

The player's mission is to uncover the secrets of this world.

---

# 5. Progression Systems

## Short-Term Progression

Every session should provide:

* Resource gain
* XP gain
* Plant growth

Expected duration:

1–5 minutes

---

## Mid-Term Progression

Players unlock:

* Evolutions
* Rare plants
* New Codex entries

Expected duration:

10–30 minutes

---

## Long-Term Progression

Players work toward:

* Full collection completion
* Legendary species
* Complete Codex

Expected duration:

Multiple sessions

# 6. Resource Economy

The game uses three primary resources.

---

## Water

Represents hydration and life.

Sources:

* Drink Water task
* Battle rewards
* Daily rewards

Uses:

* Plant growth

---

## Nutrients

Represents nourishment.

Sources:

* Exercise task
* Battle rewards

Uses:

* Growth
* Evolution requirements

---

## Sunlight

Represents energy.

Sources:

* Daily login
* Study task
* Achievements

Uses:

* Growth
* Rare evolutions

---

# 7. Growth System

Growth is the primary progression mechanic.

Players spend resources to increase plant experience.

Formula:

Growth XP =
(Water + Nutrients + Sunlight) × Growth Multiplier

---

## Growth Goals

Growth should feel:

* Immediate
* Visible
* Rewarding

Every growth action should update:

* XP bar
* Level
* Plant appearance

---

# 22. Definition of Fun

The game succeeds when players experience:

"I wonder what this plant becomes next."

and

"I want to unlock the next species."

If the game consistently creates curiosity, discovery, and attachment to plant creatures, then Rise of the Plants has achieved its design goals.
