Rise of the Plants (ROP)
Product Requirements Document

Version: 1.0

1. Executive Summary

Rise of the Plants (ROP) is a web-first augmented reality collection game where players grow magical plant creatures through task completion, progression, and discovery.

The experience combines:

Creature collection
Plant evolution
Structured lore discovery
AR interaction
Lightweight AI battles

The project is designed for the Replit + Sanity Buildathon and must clearly demonstrate the use of structured content powered by Sanity.

2. Product Goals
Primary Goal

Create a memorable collection and discovery experience that can be fully understood within three minutes.

Secondary Goal

Demonstrate meaningful use of Sanity as a content backend.

Tertiary Goal

Showcase a polished AR interaction experience.

3. Non-Goals

The MVP will NOT include:

Multiplayer
PvP
Trading
Guilds
Marketplace
Monetization
User-generated content
Native mobile applications
Push notifications
Real-world GPS mechanics
Health API integrations
Complex RPG systems
