# Rise of the Plants (ROP)

Version: 1.0
Purpose: Primary source of truth for Replit Agent implementation

---

# Mission

Build a polished, deployable web-first AR game called **Rise of the Plants (ROP)**.

ROP is a collectible creature game where players grow magical plant beings through real-world-inspired actions.

Players complete tasks, earn resources, evolve plants, unlock lore, view plants in AR, and battle AI opponents.

The experience should feel magical, rewarding, and easy to understand within minutes.

---

# Buildathon Context

This project is being built for the Replit + Sanity Buildathon.

The project must:

* Be built primarily using Replit Agent
* Use Sanity as a core content backend
* Be fully functional and deployable
* Clearly demonstrate structured content being queried from Sanity
* Deliver a memorable demo experience

---

# Product Vision

The goal is not to build the largest game.

The goal is to create a polished experience where users can:

1. Obtain a magical seed
2. Complete a task
3. Grow a plant
4. Trigger an evolution
5. Unlock lore
6. View the plant in AR
7. Battle an AI opponent
8. Discover a rare species

All within a short session.

The experience should prioritize delight over complexity.

---

# Core Fantasy

The player is a magical botanist discovering mysterious plant creatures.

Every plant has:

* A history
* A habitat
* A rarity
* An evolution path
* Unique lore

The player is building a living collection.
