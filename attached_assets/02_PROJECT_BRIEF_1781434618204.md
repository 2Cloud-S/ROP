# Rise of the Plants (ROP)

Version: 1.0

Project Type: Web-First AR Collection Game

Competition: Replit + Sanity Buildathon

---

# Executive Summary

Rise of the Plants (ROP) is a magical creature collection game where players nurture mysterious plant beings through real-world-inspired activities.

Players collect seeds, complete tasks, grow magical plants, trigger evolutions, discover lore, explore an expanding Codex, place plants into the real world through AR, and battle AI opponents.

ROP combines:

* Collection
* Discovery
* Progression
* Storytelling
* Augmented Reality

into a single experience designed to be understood and enjoyed within minutes.

The project is intentionally focused on creating a memorable and polished gameplay loop rather than a large feature set.

---

# Problem Statement

Many productivity and habit-tracking applications suffer from a common problem:

Users lose motivation quickly.

Progress often feels:

* Invisible
* Repetitive
* Meaningless

Traditional reward systems rarely create emotional attachment.

At the same time, collection-based games create strong motivation through:

* Discovery
* Progression
* Ownership
* Curiosity

ROP combines these concepts.

Instead of tracking habits for their own sake, players nurture magical plant creatures and build a living collection.

---

# Vision Statement

Create a world where every action helps bring a magical plant ecosystem to life.

The player's goal is not simply to complete tasks.

The player's goal is to discover, evolve, and understand an expanding world of plant creatures.

Each species should feel:

* Unique
* Meaningful
* Collectible
* Alive

* Multiplayer
* PvP
* Trading
* Guilds
* Marketplace
* Monetization
* Chat
* Crafting

# Definition of Success

Rise of the Plants succeeds when a first-time player experiences wonder.

If a player can grow a plant, watch it evolve, learn its story, place it into their world through AR, and feel motivated to discover the next species, then the project has achieved its goal.
