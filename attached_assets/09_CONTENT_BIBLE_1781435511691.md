# 1. The World of Verdantia

Long before humans recorded history, the world was connected by a living network known as the Verdant Weave.

The Verdant Weave was a magical force flowing beneath forests, mountains, rivers, and deserts.

From this energy emerged the first Plant Spirits.

Unlike ordinary plants, these beings possessed awareness, memory, and the ability to evolve.

Over centuries they spread across the world, adapting to different environments and forming unique species.

Today, only fragments of their ancient civilization remain.

Players take the role of a Botanist Explorer tasked with rediscovering these forgotten species and rebuilding the Grand Codex of Verdantia.

# 12. Canon Rules

All future content must follow:

1. Plants are guardians, not monsters.
2. Evolution represents growth, not corruption.
3. Discovery is more important than combat.
4. Nature and magic are interconnected.
5. Wonder should always outweigh conflict.

---

# 13. Definition of Success

The content succeeds when players:

* Remember species names
* Read Codex entries voluntarily
* Feel attached to their plants
* Want to discover the next evolution

At that point Rise of the Plants feels like a living world rather than a collection of game mechanics.
